# Benchmark results, 2026-11-04: v0.7 (Prod) vs v0.8 (Dev)

Bowerbird v0.7 (Prod, https://d3eu3eflreofpm.cloudfront.net/) against the v0.8
candidate (Dev stack newhaven-viewer-dev-20261104, module ref `dev-20261104-1` plus
the website at `release/0.8`), both live behind CloudFront, from one laptop (Chrome
146, macOS). Generated by `scripts/website/benchmark/report.mjs`; raw runs in the JSON
files. v0.8's performance-sensitive change is the scene footprints (STAC / CMR item
geometry, drawn under each loaded tile as "no data in scene").

## Summary

- **Panning**: frame times unchanged (17 ms p50 and p95) for live (Landsat, NAIP) and
  local COG (impervious surface) products.
- **Loading** a live product: time to settle within noise over 5 runs (Landsat
  6.18 → 6.47 s, NAIP 6.68 → 6.50 s). Main-thread blocking (long tasks) is higher for
  Landsat (+0.6 s), but a CPU profile of the same load shows no extra script work
  (~135 ms of JavaScript either way; none in the new code): most likely the one-time
  compile of the footprint layer's shader, plus noise in the long-task measure.
- **Local COG** load unchanged; payload +1% (13 kB).
- **STAC searches with geometry** (Node, medians of 3, Planetary Computer):

| box | dataset | items | without geometry | with geometry |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| New Haven (0.5°) | Landsat 8-9 | 1119 | 133 kB, 1.94 s | 350 kB, 2.12 s |
| New Haven (0.5°) | NAIP | 342 | 43 kB, 0.22 s | 97 kB, 0.45 s |
| Connecticut (2°, the cap) | Landsat 8-9 | 3657 | 432 kB, 5.69 s | 1134 kB, 6.11 s |
| Connecticut (2°, the cap) | NAIP | 4000 (max) | 492 kB, 2.81 s | 1124 kB, 3.13 s |

  About 2.5x the bytes (uncompressed) and +0.2-0.4 s per search; parsing stays < 5 ms.
  NAIP's 2° case is not reached on the site (NAIP's minimum zoom keeps views < 0.5°).

- **Animation**: sampling the map's center every frame for 1.5 s after each of 5 steps
  (Landsat, imagery loaded): v0.7 showed the gray background once per step (5 of 453
  samples), v0.8 never (0 of 454).
